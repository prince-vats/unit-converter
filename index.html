Equation Used

$$ C_{\mu g/m^3} = \frac{ppm \times MW \times P \times 1000}{R \times T} $$

where:

  • MW = molecular weight (g/mol)
  • P = pressure (atm)
  • T = temperature (K)
  • R = 0.082057 L·atm/mol·K

Assumptions & Notes

  • Uses ideal gas law.
  • Temperature and pressure significantly affect conversion.
  • Conversion factors at STP/NTP differ.
  • Applicable for gases, not particulate matter.
